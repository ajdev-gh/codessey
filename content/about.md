Hey there! I’m **Ajai Dev**, the builder, breaker, and occasional fixer
behind *The Codessey*. 

I’m currently an engineering student pursuing a degree in Computer Science
with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (CSE-
AIML). But if I’m being completely honest? While neural networks and data
pipelines are cool, my true technical passion lies deep within the world of
**Open Source Software (OSS)**, systems programming, and tooling. 

---

## My Current Stack & Obsessions

I like understanding how my tools work, which naturally led me down a bit
of a rabbit hole. Right now, my development workflow centers around two
main pillars:

*   **Rust:** For when I want safety, speed, and the absolute joy of fighting
    the borrow checker. I'm using it to build fast, reliable CLI tools and
    explore systems-level architecture.
*   **Nix / NixOS:** For when I want reproducible builds and declarative
    system configurations. It has a steep learning curve, but never having
    to hear *"well, it worked on my machine"* ever again is entirely worth it.

Oh, and because no developer bio is complete without mentioning it: **I use
Arch, btw.** (Though Nix is rapidly taking over my config files).

---

## Why "The Codessey"?

For me, software engineering isn't a linear path from A to B. It’s an odyssey.
It involves getting lost in documentation at 2 AM, building temporary sandbox
projects just to scratch an itch, and continuously refactoring code until it
feels right. 

This blog is my public digital garden—a place to document my journey, share my
open-source contributions, and keep myself accountable as I level up my
engineering skills.

## Let's Connect

When I'm not writing code or configuring dotfiles, I'm usually hanging out
in GitHub repositories or looking for new open-source projects to contribute to.

If you want to talk about Rust architecture, share Nix expressions, or just debate
text editors, feel free to reach out or open an issue on my repository.
