## Introducing: *Zero to $O(N)$*

To keep my data structures and algorithms (DSA) skills sharp, I am
officially launching a weekly LeetCode series. I've dubbed it **Zero to
$O(N)$**. 

The premise is simple: we are starting from the absolute fundamentals (the
"Easiest" tier) and systematically ramping up the complexity. 

> **The Philosophy:** It’s easy to copy-paste an optimal solution, but
> it’s much harder to understand *why* a brute-force $O(N^2)$ approach fails
> and how to optimize it down to $O(N)$ or $O(\log N)$. 

Every tuesday, I’ll dissect a problem, break down the intuition behind it,
analyze the time and space complexity, and provide clean, documented code.
